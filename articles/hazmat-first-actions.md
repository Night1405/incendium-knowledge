# Hazmat: First Actions

The first crews at a hazardous materials incident set the tone for the whole job. Slow down, gather information and protect people before acting.

> [!CAUTION]
> Approach from upwind and uphill. Do not enter a vapour cloud or walk through spillages.

## Step back, identify, cordon

1. **Step back:** stop short of the scene and look for signs of a release (smells, clouds, people collapsed).
2. **Identify:** read the hazard warning panel from a distance with binoculars.
3. **Cordon:** set an initial cordon and keep the public and other agencies out.

## Reading the hazard warning panel

| Panel line | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Emergency Action Code (EAC), e.g. **2X** | Firefighting medium, PPE, risk of violent reaction, contain or dilute |
| UN number, e.g. **1017** | The substance (1017 = chlorine) |
| Specialist advice number | Who to call for advice |

> [!NOTE]
> Record the UN number and EAC: the IRS report asks for them (questions 5.19 to 5.21).

## Request early

- Hazardous Materials Adviser / DIM officer.
- Additional resources for decontamination.
- Police for wider cordons and traffic.

> [!TIP]
> If in doubt, treat it as hazardous until proven otherwise.
