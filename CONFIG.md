## Who gets each app

An app only appears on a player's phone and tablet when they are allowed it. Everyone else never sees it (not even in the app store). A player gets an app when they pass its permission tier below AND (if the app's ShowWhen asks for it) they are on duty or booked on to a callsign: OnDuty    the player's duty state bag is true (Config.Duty.StateBag) BookedOn  the player is booked on to a callsign on Albo's FMS Either one is enough. Both false = whenever the permission tier passes. Players are re-checked the moment their duty state changes, when FMS bookings change, and on Qbox / QBCore job or duty changes. A tier is true (everyone), false (nobody) or a list of aces; on Qbox / QBCore it can also name jobs:  jesip = { 'incendium.phone.jesip', jobs = { 'fire' }, onDuty = true } Anyone who passes 'admin' passes every tier. Grant in server.cfg:  add_ace group.fire incendium.phone.jesip allow

## Incendium Respond: who may use each section

The permission tier (from Config.Permissions above) each section needs, on top of having the app. 'respond' = everyone who has the app. Status    status buttons, attach to an incident, radio channels Turnouts  turnout alerts, Respond mode and Pushover (in Settings) Overview  Service Overview: crewing per station, incidents in progress, CAD logs Knowledge     the knowledge base (Config.Knowledge) Hydrants      nearest hydrants, waypoints, reporting a defect HydrantReview reviewing defect reports: out of service / dismiss / back in service Ssri          viewing SSRI / PORIS sites SsriEdit      adding, editing and deleting sites, and their photos Irs           IRS reports: write your own, read your own IrsAll        read everyone's recorded IRS reports

## Incendium Respond: turnout alerts

Turnouts come from Incendium Mobilising (manual and FMS turnouts) and straight from Albo's FMS (a unit attached to an incident). Each incident alerts a player once, however many places report it. The tone loops until the player acknowledges it in the app. Each player picks a Respond mode in the app's Settings: assigned  incidents their own callsign is attached to (booked on, on the FMS) station   every turnout for the stations they pick, plus assigned ones off       no alerts Sound is played with xsound (install it; without it the alert is silent).

### `Config.Turnouts`

| Setting | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Sound = 'sounds/turnout.ogg',` | file in this resource (replace it with your own .ogg or .mp3) |
| `Volume = 0.6,` | starting volume for players (0.0 - 1.0); each player can change it |
| `DefaultMode = 'assigned',` | 'assigned', 'station' or 'off' for players who haven't chosen |
| `RepeatAfterMinutes = 30,` | the same incident doesn't alert a player again within this time |

Pushover: each officer can have turnouts sent to their real phone through their own Pushover account. They enter their own app token and user key in the app; both are kept on the server only and never sent back to anyone.

## Incendium Respond: hydrants

The hydrant list is fixed (content/hydrants.json). Defect reports and out-of-service marks are kept until the server restarts.

### `Config.Hydrants`

| Setting | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Nearest = 10,` | how many of the nearest hydrants the app lists |
| `MaxDistance = 1000,` | metres: hydrants further away are not listed |
| `ReportDistance = 150,` | metres: a defect can only be reported from this close to the hydrant |
